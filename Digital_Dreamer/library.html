Library Management

About Our Library

Our School Library module facilitates the management of books, journals, magazines, and digital resources. It allows librarians and staff to catalog new items, manage borrowing and returns, and keep track of inventory.

This system aims to promote reading and resource accessibility for students and teachers, providing a seamless experience for checking out materials and maintaining library records.

Additional features include overdue notifications, book reservations, and reports for inventory management.
