Nigerian Hospital Quality Rating System

Promoting Healthcare Excellence Through Transparency and Accountability

Advancing Healthcare Standards in Nigeria

The Nigerian Hospital Quality Rating System (NHQRS) is a comprehensive evaluation framework designed to assess and compare healthcare facilities across Nigeria. Our system empowers patients, healthcare providers, and policymakers with objective data to drive continuous improvement in healthcare delivery.

By evaluating hospitals on key performance indicators, we aim to enhance transparency, promote quality care, and ultimately improve health outcomes for all Nigerians.

Key Quality Indicators

Mortality Rates

Measures risk-adjusted mortality rates for common conditions and procedures, providing insight into the effectiveness of medical interventions and patient care.

Patient Safety

Evaluates hospital-acquired infections, surgical complications, medication errors, and other safety indicators to ensure patient well-being.

Readmission Rates

Tracks unplanned readmissions within 30 days of discharge, indicating quality of care and discharge planning effectiveness.

Patient Experience

Assesses patient satisfaction through surveys measuring communication, responsiveness, pain management, and overall care experience.

Care Timeliness

Measures waiting times for emergency care, elective procedures, and consultations, ensuring prompt access to necessary treatments.

Hospital Rating Scale

Hospitals are evaluated on a 5-star rating system based on their performance across all quality indicators. This simplified rating helps patients quickly understand a facility's overall quality while still having access to detailed metrics for each category.
