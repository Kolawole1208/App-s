Overview of the System

Our School Management System is built to simplify the administration of student records, class management, and school activities. It offers a user-friendly interface to help staff, teachers, students, and parents stay connected and informed.

Main Features

  • Add Student: Easily register new students into the system. Fill out the required fields such as name, age, class, and other personal details to keep the database updated.
  • View Students: Access the complete list of all registered students. Search, filter, and view detailed profiles to stay informed about student information.
  • Community: Engage with the school community, participate in discussions, and access school announcements.
  • Login: Secure access for staff, teachers, and authorized personnel to manage data and access specialized features.
  • Sign In / Sign Up: Authenticate your identity or create an account for personalized access.

How to Use the System

  • Navigating: Use the top menu buttons to switch between different sections. The interface is designed for ease of use.
  • Adding Students: Click on "Add Student" button to open the registration form. Fill in the details and submit to save.
  • Viewing Data: Click on "View Students" to see the list of students. Use search and filtering options.
  • Community Engagement: Participate or view notices through the Community section.
  • User Accounts & Security: Use the Login section for authorized access. Keep your login details confidential.
