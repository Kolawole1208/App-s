About This Tool

This system uses machine learning and clinical algorithms to assess diabetes risk based on established medical parameters.

The prediction model analyzes multiple factors including glucose levels, BMI, age, and family history to provide a risk assessment.

Note: This tool is for clinical decision support only and should not replace professional medical diagnosis.
